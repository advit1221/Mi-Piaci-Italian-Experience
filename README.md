# Mi Piaci Trattoria

# MI PIACI WEBSITE REDESIGN

## Complete Creative Direction + UX + Development Brief



The objective is to create a website mockup that immediately impresses the client and makes Mi Piaci feel like a real, character-rich Italian restaurant brand rather than a generic café website.

The website should primarily encourage visitors to **explore the Mi Piaci brand**, discover its food, understand its story, see its spaces, find its three locations, browse the menu, and eventually make a reservation.

---

# 1. CORE CREATIVE DIRECTION

The visual direction is:

## RUSTIC ITALIAN TRATTORIA

Think:

* intimate Italian neighbourhood restaurant

* handmade pasta

* flour-covered preparation tables

* wood-fired pizza

* warm ovens

* aged timber

* dark espresso

* terracotta

* cream menus

* handwritten notes

* Italian family restaurants

* old menu cards

* Italian typography

* imperfect handmade details

* warm evening lighting

* linen tablecloths

* ceramic plates

* olive oil

* tomatoes

* basil

* wine bottles

* vintage Italian signage

* trattoria storefronts

* Mediterranean warmth

* contemporary editorial photography

It should feel as though a small piece of Italy exists inside Delhi/Gurgaon.

The website should NOT feel like:

* a generic SaaS website

* a corporate restaurant chain

* a minimalist tech startup

* a generic beige luxury café

* an ultra-clean template

* a glassmorphism-heavy website

* a futuristic website

* a sterile fine-dining website

* an overly modern black-and-white fashion site

The design can be sophisticated, but sophistication should come from **composition, typography, photography, movement, texture and restraint**, not excessive minimalism.

The visitor should almost be able to imagine the smell of pizza coming from the oven.

Typography is extremely important.

Use an editorial Italian-inspired serif for major headings.

* Italian restaurant menus

* vintage European print

* traditional publishing

* Italian cinema posters

Pair this with a clean, highly readable sans-serif for:

* body text

* navigation

* menu descriptions

* buttons

* location information

Optionally introduce a restrained handwritten/script typeface ONLY for tiny decorative phrases.

Do not overuse script fonts.

Typography should have personality.

Use:

* oversized editorial headlines

* elegant italic serif words

* small uppercase labels

* generous whitespace

* deliberate line breaks

* asymmetrical text layouts

Avoid making every heading perfectly centered.
